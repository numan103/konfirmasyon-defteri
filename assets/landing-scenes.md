# Landing story scenes

Custom, locally authored SVG illustrations that describe the product flow.
They are representative artwork, not screenshots, live data, or images of real users.

- `landing-workspace-scene.svg`: multi-monitor trading desk (İşlem)
- `landing-research-scene.svg`: onchain data flow and market bias (Veri)
- `landing-community-scene.svg`: trader community discussion (Topluluk)

Each scene is inline SVG with a `<title>`, no external requests, and no text that
could be read as live performance data. They are decorative support for the story
section; the same concepts are described in the adjacent copy.
